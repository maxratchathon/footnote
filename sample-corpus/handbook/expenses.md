# Expenses

Spend company money the way you would spend your own when it matters. You do not need pre-approval for anything under 150 EUR.

## Submitting an expense

Submit expenses in Ledgerline within 45 days of the purchase. Attach a receipt that shows the vendor, date and amount. Expenses submitted after 45 days need sign-off from Finance and may be rejected.

Reimbursements are paid with the next monthly payroll run, as long as they are approved by the 20th of the month.

## What needs pre-approval

Ask your manager before spending more than 150 EUR in one purchase, and ask Finance before any software subscription, whatever the price. Subscriptions must go through Finance so we can track renewals and avoid duplicate tools.

## Travel

Book travel through Ledgerline's travel tab when possible. Economy class is the default. For flights longer than eight hours, premium economy is allowed. The daily meal allowance while travelling is 60 EUR, and alcohol is not reimbursed.

## Learning budget

Everyone has a yearly learning budget of 1,000 EUR for books, courses and conferences. Unused budget does not roll over to the next year.
