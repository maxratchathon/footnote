# Remote Work

Halyard Labs is remote-first. We have one office, in Leith, which anyone can use, but no team is required to be there.

## Core hours

Everyone overlaps between 14:00 and 17:00 UTC on weekdays. Meetings involving more than one time zone should be scheduled inside core hours. Outside core hours, work whenever suits you.

## Home office budget

New starters get a one-time home office budget of 1,800 EUR for a desk, chair, monitor and anything else that makes their setup work. After that, there is a yearly refresh allowance of 400 EUR. Equipment bought with the budget belongs to you.

## Coworking

If you prefer working outside your home, Halyard Labs pays up to 250 EUR per month for a coworking membership. Submit the invoice as an expense (see the Expenses page).

## Team gatherings

The whole company meets in person twice a year, in spring and autumn, for a week-long gathering. Travel and accommodation are booked and paid for by the company. Teams may also hold one extra team-only meetup per year, with a budget of 900 EUR per person.
