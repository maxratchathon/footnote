# Time Off

Halyard Labs does not track vacation days in a fixed bank. Instead, everyone gets a minimum of 22 working days off per calendar year, and managers check in with anyone who has taken fewer than 10 days by the end of August.

## Requesting time off

Request time off in the #out-of-office channel and add it to the shared "Away" calendar. For anything longer than five consecutive working days, give at least three weeks' notice so the team can plan around it.

You do not need approval for single days. Longer breaks need a thumbs-up from your manager, which is mostly about coverage, not permission.

## Public holidays

We observe the public holidays of the country you live in. If you work on a public holiday (for example during an on-call shift), take a replacement day within the next 60 days.

## Sick leave

Sick leave is separate from time off and has no limit. Post in #out-of-office that you are sick; no details are needed. After five consecutive sick days, People Ops will reach out to check whether you need longer-term support.

## Sabbatical

After four full years at Halyard Labs, you can take a paid six-week sabbatical. It must be taken within 18 months of becoming eligible and cannot be split into more than two blocks.
