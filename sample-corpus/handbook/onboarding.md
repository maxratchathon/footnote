# Onboarding

Your first two weeks at Halyard Labs follow the same plan, whatever your role.

## Week one

On day one you meet your manager and your onboarding buddy. The buddy is someone from a different team who answers the questions you might not want to ask your manager. You will get access to Slack, Ledgerline, the Tidewater staging environment and the internal wiki.

By the end of week one, you should have Tidewater running locally and have shipped a small change, usually a docs fix. Engineers pair with their buddy on their first pull request.

## Week two

In week two, you shadow one support shift and one on-call handover, regardless of your role. This is how everyone learns what customers actually struggle with.

## 30-day check-in

After 30 days, you and your manager fill in a short written check-in: what is going well, what is confusing, and what you want to work on next. There is no rating or score.
