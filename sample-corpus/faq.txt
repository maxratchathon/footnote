Halyard Labs FAQ

Who founded Halyard Labs?
Halyard Labs was founded in 2019 by Ines Carvalho and Tomasz Wrona, who previously built asset pipelines for an animation studio. The company has around 70 employees.

Where is the office?
The only office is in Leith, Edinburgh. It has eight desks and a meeting room, and is open to all staff.

Which tools does the company use?
Slack for chat, Ledgerline for expenses and travel, Linear for issue tracking, and the internal wiki for documentation.

Who do I ask about payroll?
Payroll questions go to People Ops in the #people channel. Expense questions go to Finance in #finance.

How do I report a security issue?
Email security@halyard.example or post in the private #security channel. Do not open a public ticket.
